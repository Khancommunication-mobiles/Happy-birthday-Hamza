Chapter Eight · The Letter

Everything we never said out loud

Some words take a whole universe to carry them. This one took ten chapters — and it was always meant for you, Hamza.

My dearest Hamza,

Happy Birthday! Tonight the moon has a reason to shine a little brighter, because it is your day — the day that gave us you.

They say this is your last birthday with us, and I will not pretend that word — "last" — does not sting. But here is what I know: "last" is only about addresses and calendars. It has never once applied to us. Some bonds are not measured in distance; they are measured in laughs per day, and ours has always broken the scale.

Thank you for every laugh, every secret, every 2 a.m. conversation, and every ordinary day you turned into a memory. Hamza × Anmol is still my favourite team, and no change of city, timezone or occasion is ever going to change that roster.

So tonight, make the loudest wish. Blow out the candles like you mean it. And know that wherever you go, you carry us with you — quietly, loyally, forever — exactly the way you carried us all along.

Forever yours, Anmol
